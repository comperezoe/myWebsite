<h2> <em>Zoé Compère is a recitalist, soloist, an orchestra musician and a chamber musician. Through various repertoires, she weaves a link between music from past to present. </em></h2>

Zoé Compère is a Belgium-based flutist
who aims to reconcile all repertoires,
from renaissance to contemporary, and
share her passion for the instrument with
her audience. 

She has performed in many
contexts : in recital, as a soloist with
various Belgian orchestras, in chamber
music ensembles and orchestras.She has won many prizes at competitions
such as : Sonatina Competition in 2022
(1st prize), Micha Competition in 2023
(2nd prize and audience prize) and
Concours Luxembourgeois pour jeunes
solistes in 2024 (silver medal).
During 2025-2026, she played with the
Orchestre Philharmonique Royal in Liege
as an academist.


Zoé has performed as a soloist with the
Orchestre de la Force Aerienne in
Belgium as well as the orchestra "Nuove
Musiche".

Having studied with Denis-Pierre Gustin at the Institut Royal Supérieur de Musique et Pédagogie in Namur,
she is currently pursuing her master
degree with Valerie Debaele at the
Maastricht Conservatorium.
